### Everyday things

- **Change the menu:** Start here → Change the menu.
- **Change colours or fonts:** Start here → Colours and fonts. Try a whole look under "Browse styles". Nothing is permanent: there's an undo and a "Reset".

### Good habits
- Describe every photo when you add it (the "Alternative text" box): one short sentence about what it shows. It helps people who can't see the photo, and search engines.
- Use headings in order: "Heading 2" for sections, "Heading 3" inside them. Make text bigger with the size setting, not with a bigger heading.
- Links say where they go: "see our opening hours", not "click here".
- Photos straight from a phone are fine; the site makes smaller copies itself.
- Only use photos you took or have permission to use.

### What not to worry about
- Updates and security are handled by WordPress.com.
- You can't break the design by editing texts and photos. If something looks wrong, undo (Ctrl/Cmd + Z), or close without saving.
